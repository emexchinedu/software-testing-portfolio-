## Test levels
| # | Level | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | System | Tester | To check if customer can order from the app successfully
| 2 | Unit | Developer | To make sure it doesn't give the wrong number
| 3 | Acceptance | Product owner | To make sure the menu is complete with customer needs
| 4 | Integration | Developer or Tester | To make sure the order is received 
| 5 | Unit | Developer | To make sure the customer gets to the dashboard
| 6 | Acceptance | Real Users | To make order from the app
| 7 | Unit | Developer | To make sure the security is on for the safety of an account
| 8 | System | Tester | To make sure the customer can access the account and make payment
